Synthetic degradations occur after preprocessing, including padded pixels; not a calibrated camera pipeline.
Two low-light settings change brightness, NOT blur-kernel strength. They are not motion-blur tests.
Validation uses the same two corruption families as training; gains are in-family evidence.
No inference restoration module was added. Same architecture in both runs.
Legacy yolo26s_balanced filename refers to YOLOv9s noise run, not a YOLO26 model.
Resume changed RNG trajectory; one seed and no uninterrupted matched rerun. Results are preliminary.
